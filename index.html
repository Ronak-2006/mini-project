Every worker deserves to go home safe. Below are the essential Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) items and general safety precautions every construction site must follow.

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

Hard Hat

Protects the head from falling objects, bumps, or electrical shocks. Essential in overhead work areas.

General Safety Tips

  • Inspect tools and equipment before use.
  • Keep worksite clear of tripping hazards.
  • Follow proper lifting techniques.
  • Stay hydrated during hot weather.
  • Use barricades and safety signs.
  • Never disable machine safety guards.

First Aid Kit & Emergency Procedures

Every site must have a stocked first aid kit and trained personnel. Include the following:

  • Bandages, gauze, and adhesive tape
  • Burn cream and antiseptic wipes
  • Pain relievers (aspirin, ibuprofen)
  • Medical gloves and sterile pads
  • Emergency contact list
